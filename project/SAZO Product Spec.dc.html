Know the car before you buy it.

SAZO turns a number plate into a sourced, timestamped history — repairs, accidents, inspections, mileage and ownership — contributed by the garages, dealers and inspection centres that actually touched the vehicle.

01

Product overview

Vision

Every used-car sale in the region happens with the same information on both sides of the table. A buyer should be able to verify a seller’s claims in two minutes, from a phone, standing next to the car.

Value proposition

One plate. Every verified record. Clearly sourced, honestly incomplete.

Buyers

A plain-language history with every claim traced to who recorded it and when — and an explicit list of what SAZO could not find.

Garages

A fast job-card tool that doubles as a public credential: “Serviced by a SAZO Verified garage” sells their work.

Dealers

Inventory with shareable, verified history links that shorten sales conversations and justify pricing.

Inspection centres

Structured result upload that replaces paper certificates and reduces fraudulent copies.

Product principles

  1. Source before score. Every fact shows its contributor, date and verification level. No unsourced summary is ever shown.
  2. Absence is not evidence. “No accident records found” is never shown as “No accidents”.
  3. Calm by default, direct when it matters. Red is reserved for findings a buyer must act on.
  4. Contributing must be faster than paper. A garage record takes under 90 seconds.

User personas

Buyer · primary

Amina, 31 — first private purchase

Buying from a stranger online with savings. Not a car expert; checks on her phone at the viewing.

Goals: spot red flags fast; know what to ask the seller; not overpay.

Fears: rolled-back mileage, hidden accident, being misled by jargon.

Buyer · power

David, 44 — family upgrade, comparing 3 cars

Shortlists on desktop in the evening, wants side-by-side facts.

Goals: compare like for like; save cars; get alerts if a record changes.

Fears: paying for reports on cars that turn out empty.

Garage

Joseph — owner, 6-bay independent garage

Works on a shared tablet in the workshop, gloves on, poor Wi-Fi.

Goals: log jobs in seconds; give mechanics limited access; show reputation.

Fears: admin overhead, liability for a customer’s dispute.

Dealer

Grace — sales manager, 120-car lot

Manages stock intake, prep and listings across two branches.

Goals: history on every car at intake; shareable links on listings; see what buyers view.

Fears: a damaging record surfacing after she has priced a car.

Inspection centre

Peter — lane supervisor

Runs 60+ inspections a day; results already live in lane equipment.

Goals: bulk upload; certificate generation; prevent forged certificates.

Fears: double entry, mistakes under time pressure.

SAZO admin

Lina — trust & data quality lead

Approves partners, resolves disputes, watches for anomalies.

Goals: a queue ordered by risk; full audit trail; bulk actions.

Fears: a bad partner poisoning thousands of reports.

05

Core user journeys

Happy path in four to six steps. Key decision or risk point noted per journey.

A · Search by plate

  1. Enters plate on home (input auto-uppercases, strips spaces, validates format live).
  2. Optional: confirms region if the format is ambiguous.
  3. Sees match card: make, model, year, colour, photo-free silhouette — “Is this the car you’re looking at?”
  4. Confirms → free overview. Mismatch → “Check the plate” help and VIN search.

Risk point: wrong-car confusion. The colour + model confirmation step prevents paying for the wrong vehicle.

B · Review a history report

  1. Lands on Summary: findings list sorted by severity, coverage meter, “What we couldn’t check”.
  2. Taps a finding → jumps to its section with the record expanded.
  3. Each record shows source, verification badge, date recorded vs date of event.
  4. Ends on “Questions to ask the seller”, generated from findings; can save, share or download PDF.

C · Compare vehicles

  1. “Add to compare” from any overview or saved list (max 3 on desktop, 2 on mobile).
  2. Comparison grid: findings first, then mileage/km per year, owners, service regularity, specs.
  3. Rows where cars differ meaningfully are marked; “Hide identical rows” toggle.
  4. Unlock full report per column, or all at a bundle price.

D · Purchase a full report

  1. Overview shows locked sections with honest counts: “14 service records · 1 accident record”.
  2. Choose Single report / 3-pack / Dealer plan. Price shown before sign-in.
  3. Pay with card or mobile money; account created by email or phone OTP.
  4. Report unlocks instantly; receipt + 30 days of free updates and alerts.

If a vehicle has zero verified records, purchase is disabled and the buyer is told so — never sell an empty report.

E · Garage adds a repair record

  1. “New record” → plate (or scan plate with camera) → vehicle confirmed.
  2. Record type: Service · Repair · Accident repair · Diagnostic. Odometer reading is mandatory.
  3. Pick work items from a catalogue (parts, labour) with free-text notes.
  4. Attach invoice/photos; tick customer-consent box; submit. Offline drafts sync later.
  5. Record shows as “Partner-submitted” immediately; becomes “Verified” after automated checks.

F · Dealer manages inventory

  1. Adds stock by plate or CSV; SAZO runs an intake check on each car.
  2. Inventory table flags cars with new findings since intake.
  3. Adds prep work and photos; marks Listed / Reserved / Sold.
  4. Generates a share link or QR for listings; sees view count per car.
  5. On Sold, ownership transfer is recorded with buyer consent.

G · Centre uploads inspection

  1. Single: plate → checklist by system (brakes, lights, emissions…) → Pass / Advisory / Fail per item.
  2. Bulk: CSV or lane-equipment export → mapping preview → row-level validation errors.
  3. Inspector signs; SAZO issues a certificate with QR verification.
  4. Result goes live as “Verified source” immediately (centres are pre-accredited).

H · Admin reviews data

  1. Queue sorted by risk: mileage rollback flags, disputes, new partner applications, anomalies.
  2. Opens item → side-by-side evidence (record, documents, prior records, partner history).
  3. Approve · Request info · Correct · Reject — reason required, contributor notified.
  4. Every action written to an immutable audit log; affected report owners alerted.

08

Vehicle health summary

SAZO does not publish a single 0–100 score. A score implies completeness we can’t guarantee and invites buyers to skip the detail. Instead the summary is a findings list plus a coverage meter.

Act on this

Mileage inconsistency, structural damage, open safety recall, failed inspection, reported stolen/written off.

Worth checking

Repaired moderate accident, advisories, service gap > 12 mo, frequent ownership changes.

Consistent

Regular servicing, mileage consistent across sources, latest inspection passed.

Not checked / no records

Categories with no data. Always listed — never hidden.

  • Each finding = what + when + who recorded it + why it matters + link to evidence.
  • Coverage meter: “Records from 7 sources covering 2019–2026 · 1 gap of 14 months”. Not a grade.
  • Severity is rule-based and published in Help (“How we classify findings”), never ML-opaque.
  • Neutral verbs: “Recorded”, “Reported by”, “Not found” — never “Clean”, “Safe”, “Perfect”.
  • Persistent disclaimer under the summary: “SAZO shows what partners have recorded. Always have the car inspected before you buy.”

09

Trust and transparency

Badge Meaning
✓ Verified sourceFrom an accredited partner and passed automated checks (plate/VIN match, odometer plausibility, document present).
◆ Partner-submittedFrom a registered partner; checks pending or no supporting document.
○ Owner-submittedAdded by a vehicle owner. Shown in a separate list, never counted in findings.
! Under reviewA dispute or anomaly is open. Record stays visible with the reason shown.

Source attribution

Partner name, type, location and accreditation on every record; tap for partner profile.

Two timestamps

“Event date” and “Recorded on”. Back-dated entries (> 30 days) are labelled.

Confidence level

Per section: High (≥ 2 independent verified sources agree), Medium (1 verified), Low (partner/owner only). Shown as text + 3-bar glyph.

Missing information

“What we couldn’t check” block on every report: finance, theft, import history if not available in that market.

Report freshness

“Report generated 12 Oct 2026, 14:02. Data current to …” printed on PDF and screen.

Change log

Corrections are visible: “Record amended 3 Feb 2026 after review” — never silently edited.

10

Search, filters, alerts and reporting

Search

Plate (normalised, fuzzy on O/0, I/1 with “Did you mean”), VIN, partner-side search by plate, customer ref, staff, date range. Recent searches stored locally only.

Filters

Report: record type, source, verification level, date range. Partner lists: status, branch, staff, verification state. Admin: risk type, partner, age in queue. Active filters shown as removable chips.

Sorting

Timelines default newest-first with toggle; findings by severity; tables by any column with visible sort direction (aria-sort).

Alerts (buyers)

Watch a saved car: new record, new finding, inspection due, dispute outcome. Free for 30 days after purchase.

Notifications

Bell with unread count; grouped by day; each actionable. Severity-coded with text label. Digest option for partners.

Reporting

Buyer: PDF report with QR authenticity check. Partner: CSV/PDF exports of records & analytics. Admin: coverage, quality and dispute reports, scheduled.

12

Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Perceivable

  • Text ≥ 4.5:1; UI and large text ≥ 3:1. Status uses icon + label + colour — never colour alone.
  • Charts have a data-table alternative and a written summary.
  • Damage diagrams carry text lists of affected zones.

Operable

  • Full keyboard path; visible 2 px focus ring; skip-to-report link.
  • No timeouts on forms; drafts autosave.
  • Targets ≥ 24 px (2.5.8), 44 px on touch.

Understandable

  • Plain language, reading age ~12; glossary tooltips for “advisory”, “odometer”.
  • Errors say what happened and how to fix it, next to the field.
  • Consistent navigation across roles.

Robust

  • Semantic HTML; tables for tabular data; ARIA live regions for search results and upload progress.
  • Tested with VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA; 200% zoom and 320 px reflow.
  • Respects reduced motion.

13

Privacy, security and consent

Vehicle ≠ person

Reports describe the vehicle only. Owners appear as “Owner 2 · private · Mar 2021 – Aug 2023”. Names, phone numbers, addresses and invoice amounts are never shown to buyers.

Consent at source

Partners confirm the customer was informed (job card checkbox + signage kit). Owners can request redaction of personal data inside documents.

Data minimisation

Uploads auto-redact faces/other plates in photos and strip EXIF location. Cost fields stored but partner-private.

Access control

Role-based permissions, branch scoping, 2FA for managers and admins, session timeouts on partner devices, admin actions require reason codes.

Security

TLS 1.3, encryption at rest, signed records (hash per entry), immutable audit log, rate-limited plate search to prevent scraping.

Rights & compliance

Export / delete account, alert opt-outs, cookie choices with equal-weight Reject. Aligned to local data protection law and GDPR principles; retention schedule published.

Dispute / correction flow

1

Flag a record

“Something wrong?” on every record. Who: buyer, owner, or partner.

2

Describe + evidence

Reason list, free text, upload documents. Owners verify via ownership doc.

3

Under review

Record badged; contributor has 5 days to respond.

4

Admin decision

Upheld · Corrected · Rejected, with written reason.

5

Notify & log

All report holders alerted; change log visible on the record.

17

MVP and roadmap

MVP · 0–4 mo

  • Plate search + confirm + free overview
  • Full report: summary, repairs, accidents, inspections, mileage, ownership periods
  • Single-report purchase, PDF
  • Garage + inspection centre consoles (single entry)
  • Verification badges, sources, two timestamps
  • Dispute form + admin review queue, audit log
  • Staff roles (Owner / Contributor)

Next · 4–8 mo

  • Dealer console, inventory, share links
  • Compare (3 cars), saved vehicles, alerts
  • Bulk CSV upload for centres
  • Offline drafts, plate camera scan
  • Full permissions model + branches
  • Partner analytics

Later · 8–18 mo

  • Garage DMS & lane-equipment APIs
  • Insurer, finance and theft-register data
  • Valuation guidance from history
  • Owner app: claim car, add records, service reminders
  • Public partner profiles & reviews
  • Multi-language, additional markets
