Flat, ruled, and honest about uncertainty.

Modernist structure — Archivo, 0 px radius, 2 px rules, flush-left labels, visible grid — keyed to SAZO’s blue, green and white. Colour carries meaning, so it is spent sparingly.

Colour

Ramps generated in OKLCH on a shared lightness scale. 100–300 fills · 500/600 base · 700–900 text on tints.

Alerts and high-risk information

What · when · who · why · next step. Mileage dropped 12,500 km between Jan and Jun 2024. Ask for invoices.

Worth checking

Accident repaired, 2022. No structural damage recorded.

Info

Report updated 10 Oct — 1 new service record.

Not available

Finance checks aren’t available in this region yet.

Do

  • State the fact and its source first; interpretation second.
  • Give one concrete next step (“Ask for…”, “Have it inspected”).
  • Show high-risk items at the top of the summary, always expanded.
  • Use icon + label + colour together.

Don’t

  • Say “fraud”, “dangerous” or “clean” — say what was recorded.
  • Use red for marketing, prices or countdowns.
  • Hide a finding behind a paywall entirely — its existence is always free.
  • Stack more than one red block; group findings in a single panel.

Charts

  • Blue line, square markers; hollow = verified, filled red = inconsistent.
  • Gaps drawn as shaded “No records” bands with dashed line — never interpolated silently.
  • Direct labels over legends; every chart has a table view and a one-line summary.
  • Ownership as a segmented bar; never pie charts.

Icons & illustration

  • Lucide, 2 px stroke, square caps, 20/24 px. Always paired with a text label in status contexts.
  • Illustration: flat geometric vehicle diagrams (top/side view) for damage zones only. No stock-photo cars, no mascots.
  • Partner photos print as uploaded; marketing photography in black and white.
